Average Selling Price

Calculate revenue per unit using the price on each purchase date.

Easy50 XP

Problem Statement

Return product_id and average_price for each product. Compute total sales revenue divided by total units sold, rounded to two decimal places. Products with no sold units should have an average price of 0. Any row order is accepted.

SQL Solution

Match sales to the correct product and inclusive price period. SUM(price × units) is revenue; divide it by SUM(units), group by product, and round once at the end. LEFT JOIN keeps unsold products, and COALESCE supplies 0 for their missing average.

Why this query works

SELECT p.product_id,
       ROUND(COALESCE(SUM(p.price * u.units) / SUM(u.units), 0), 2) AS average_price
FROM Prices AS p
LEFT JOIN UnitsSold AS u
ON p.product_id = u.product_id
AND u.purchase_date BETWEEN p.start_date AND p.end_date
GROUP BY p.product_id;
