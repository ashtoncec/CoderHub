Product Sales Analysis I

Match each sale to its product name using the shared product ID.

Easy50 XP

Problem Statement

For every sales record, report product_name, year, and price. Results may appear in any order.

Sales schema
ColumnType
sale_idint
product_idint
yearint
quantityint
priceint

The pair (sale_id, year) is the primary key. Sales.product_id references Product.product_id. Price is the cost per unit.

Product schema
ColumnType
product_idint
product_namevarchar

Product.product_id is the primary key, identifying one product name.

Example input: Sales
sale_idproduct_idyearquantityprice
11002008105000
21002009125000
72002011159000
Example input: Product
product_idproduct_name
100Nokia
200Apple
300Samsung
Expected output
product_nameyearprice
Nokia20085000
Nokia20095000
Apple20119000

Both Nokia sales remain separate. Samsung has no sales record, so it does not appear. Report the unit price, not quantity multiplied by price.

Open problem 1068 on LeetCode

SQL Solution

Why this query works

SELECT p.product_name, s.year, s.price
FROM Sales AS s
JOIN Product AS p
ON s.product_id = p.product_id;
