Recyclable and Low Fat Products

Select the products that meet two conditions at once.

Easy50 XP

Problem Statement

Find the IDs of products that are both low fat and recyclable. Return the result in any order.

Products schema
ColumnType
product_idint
low_fatsenum ('Y', 'N')
recyclableenum ('Y', 'N')

product_id is the primary key. Both flags use 'Y' for yes and 'N' for no.

Example input: Products
product_idlow_fatsrecyclable
0YN
1YY
2NY
3YY
4NN
Expected output
product_id
1
3

Only products 1 and 3 have 'Y' in both columns.

Open problem 1757 on LeetCode

SQL Solution

Why this query works

SELECT product_id
FROM Products
WHERE low_fats = 'Y' AND recyclable = 'Y';
