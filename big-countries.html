Big Countries

Keep countries that meet either size threshold.

Easy50 XP

Problem Statement

Return name, population, and area for countries with area at least 3,000,000 km² or population at least 25,000,000. Any row order is accepted.

World schema
ColumnType
namevarchar
continentvarchar
areaint
populationint

The country name is the primary key. Each row describes its continent, area, population, and GDP.

Example input: World

AlbaniaEurope28748283174112960000000

AndorraEurope468781153712000000
AngolaAfrica124670020609294100990000000
Expected output
namepopulationarea
Afghanistan25500100652230
Algeria371000002381741

Afghanistan and Algeria qualify through population. A country can also qualify through area alone.

Open problem 595 on LeetCode

SQL Solution

Select the three requested columns. OR accepts either threshold, and >= includes countries exactly at a threshold.

Why this query works

SELECT name, population, area
FROM World
WHERE area >= 3000000 OR population >= 25000000;
