Article Views I

Find authors who viewed their own articles.

Easy50 XP

Problem Statement

List each author who viewed at least one of their own articles. Name the output column id and sort it in ascending order.

Views schema
ColumnType
article_idint
author_idint
viewer_idint
view_datedate

There is no primary key, and duplicate rows are possible. Matching author_id and viewer_id means the author viewed their own article.

Example input: Views
article_idauthor_idviewer_idview_date
1352019-08-01
1362019-08-02
2772019-08-01
2762019-08-02
4712019-07-22
3442019-07-21
3442019-07-21
Expected output
id
4
7

Authors 4 and 7 viewed their own articles. Author 4 appears once despite duplicate views, and the IDs are sorted.

Open problem 1148 on LeetCode

SQL Solution

Filter to self-views, use DISTINCT to remove duplicate authors, rename author_id to id with AS, then sort with ORDER BY.

Why this query works

SELECT DISTINCT author_id AS id
FROM Views
WHERE author_id = viewer_id
ORDER BY id ASC;
